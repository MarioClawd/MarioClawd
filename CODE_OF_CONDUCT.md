# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone who contributes to or interacts with the MarioClawd project.

## Our Standards

**Positive behavior includes:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

**Unacceptable behavior includes:**
- Harassment, trolling, or derogatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces — GitHub, Discord, social media, and any other channels associated with MarioClawd. It also applies to agent integrations and automated interactions within the platform.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated, and will result in a response deemed appropriate to the circumstances.
